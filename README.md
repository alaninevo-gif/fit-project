## What we collect and why

Israeli supermarket chains must publish their current prices and promotions every day, but only
the current files are online, so the history is lost. Once a day we collect the full price and
promotion files of two large branches in Rishon LeZion: one of **Shufersal** (a mainstream chain)
and one of **Rami Levy** (a discount chain). We also collect the Bank of Israel USD/ILS rate as
context. Our goal is to compare the chains' promotion strategies: whether their "deals" are really
cheaper than the regular price, which chain runs more and deeper promotions, whether this
changes around Hanukkah, and whether the dollar rate affects promotions or base prices.

The sources, access methods and samples are described in [SOURCE.md](SOURCE.md).
